# CardioTrack CT-200 Home Blood Pressure Monitor

Document ID: CT200-IFU | Revision: 1.1

## 1. Intended Use

The CT-200 is intended for non-invasive measurement of systolic and diastolic blood pressure and pulse rate in adults aged 18 years or older. It is intended for home use by one patient.

### Contraindications

Do not use on neonates, children, pregnant patients, or patients with implanted electrical devices unless directed by a clinician.

## 2. Safety Information

### Warnings

- Do not adjust medication based only on a CT-200 reading.
- Stop measurement if the cuff causes pain or numbness.
- Keep the device away from water.

#### Cuff pressure protection

If cuff pressure exceeds 295 mmHg, the device shall show error `E3` and begin automatic deflation within 1 second.

### Warnings

Do not use the monitor near MRI equipment or in an oxygen-rich environment. This intentionally duplicated heading is a separate section.

## 3. Setup and Operation

### Before measurement

Rest for at least 5 minutes. Avoid caffeine, exercise, smoking, and bathing for 30 minutes before measurement.

##### Applying the cuff

Wrap the cuff on bare skin, 2-3 cm above the elbow. The lower edge must align with the artery marker. This heading intentionally skips levels 4.

### Taking a measurement

1. Sit with feet flat and back supported.
2. Place the arm at heart level.
3. Press START/STOP once.
4. Remain still until the reading appears.

Expected display fields:

| Field | Unit |
| --- | --- |
| SYS/DIA | mmHg |
| PULSE | beats/min |

## 4. Error Messages

Error reference
---------------

`E1` means the cuff is loose. `E2` means movement was detected. `E3` means over-pressure protection activated. `E5` means no valid pulse was detected.

## 5. Performance Specifications

Measurement range is 40-260 mmHg for pressure and 40-180 beats/min for pulse. Pressure accuracy is +/-3 mmHg under laboratory conditions.

### Operating environment

Operate between 10 C and 40 C, at 15%-90% relative humidity, non-condensing.

### Battery life

Four alkaline AA batteries shall support at least 300 measurements under normal conditions.

## 6. Cleaning and Storage

Clean the monitor with a soft cloth lightly dampened with water. Do not immerse it or use abrasive cleaners. Store between -20 C and 60 C.

## 7. Maintenance

Inspect the cuff and tube monthly. Replace damaged accessories with manufacturer-approved parts. The expected service life is 5 years or 10,000 measurements.
